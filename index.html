Day 3

Grinding CTF & Military Party

Written by: Noah Chang

We started our day off socializing with the Swedish students and the teachers. By now we already know who they are, but we decided we wanted to know them better. Here is a picture with our teacher and friend Johan; on this day we got to know him better. One of the things we remember from this interaction is that he told us his 2 daughters are our age ;).

We also worked on this CTF this day. The challenge that stood out most for me is the healthpotion challenge. You had to do the command man healthpotion and then got an OVA file. This file can be put into VMware and boot up a Debian Linux machine. Then on that machine, there were a lot of pieces to find and when you puzzle them together you get the flag. An amazing CTF challenge.

After school we were all hungry. We all stayed in different accommodations so we split up. I went back to my Airbnb and cooked some pasta for me and my roommate.

In the evening there was a bar event the Swedish students organized so we assembled again with the one and only, legendary, Nico to go to the bar.

After the student bar we went to the city to meet new people. There we ran into a platoon of military people that were friendly (at first). We made new friends and had a lot of friendly & funny interactions with them.

After a long day of partying we all went our separate ways again to go rest for the next day. Namaste my dudes.
